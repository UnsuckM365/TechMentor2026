# 🚀 This Month's Microsoft 365 Highlights

*Window: **August 2026** — every Microsoft 365 Roadmap item with Preview, General Availability, Current Channel, or Targeted Release activity dated this month. Current month determined automatically from today's date (August 4, 2026).*

- ⭐ **Microsoft 365: The Next Generation of File & Folder Sharing** (✅ GA, Aug 2026) — A rebuilt sharing experience spanning Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneDrive, and SharePoint, rolling out across Worldwide, GCC, GCC High, and DoD. It's the broadest single UX change this month — nearly every end user will notice the new share dialog.
- ⭐ **Microsoft Copilot (Microsoft 365): Copilot Chat in Outlook expands to reason over inbox, calendar, and enterprise data** (✅ GA, Aug 2026) — Copilot Chat can now reason over a user's full inbox, calendar, and other enterprise data *without requiring a paid Microsoft 365 Copilot license*. That widens who gets meaningful AI value in Outlook and raises fresh data-governance questions for admins.
- ⭐ **Outlook: New Copilot entry point in Classic Outlook** (👀 Current Channel + ✅ GA, Aug 2026) — A single, consistent Copilot entry point replaces scattered touchpoints across classic Outlook for Windows. Expect a wave of "where did this button go" help-desk tickets.
- ⭐ **Microsoft Purview: Sensitivity label grouping modernization** (✅ GA, Aug 2026 — GCC/GCC High/DoD) — The underlying sensitivity-labeling scheme is being simplified to labels plus label groupings. This is a structural change to how admins configure and reason about MIP labels in government clouds, not a cosmetic tweak.
- ⭐ **Microsoft 365: Introducing Exchange Online Cross-tenant Message Recall** (✅ GA, Aug 2026) — Message recall now works across tenant boundaries, not just inside your own org. High visibility, high "wait, they can do that?" support-desk potential.

## 👀 What Admins Should Watch

**Microsoft 365 administrators** — The biggest configuration lift this month is on the Purview side: DLP for SharePoint/Exchange gets several new controls (adaptive scopes, granular classification errors, rule-based alert tagging, FTP/SFTP endpoint coverage), and the sensitivity-label scheme itself is being restructured into label groupings. Review DLP and label policies before GA lands rather than after. Separately, Teams admin center and Microsoft 365 admin center are converging on unified agent/app installation management — expect one more place your existing app-permission policies need re-validating.

**Adoption and change-management teams** — Three end-user-facing UI changes will generate the most questions this month: the new Microsoft 365 file/folder sharing experience (⭐), the new unified Copilot entry point in classic Outlook (⭐), and Outlook's new "All Accounts" unified inbox view. All three change something people do daily. A short heads-up email or Viva Engage post before GA usually cuts ticket volume noticeably.

**Governance and compliance leads** — Cross-tenant message recall, sensitivity labels extending to Entra security groups, and new Purview Data Lifecycle Management retention/insights capabilities all touch data governance policy directly. None of these are opt-out after GA in most tenants, so confirm whether your existing DLP, retention, and labeling policies need updates *before* rollout rather than reacting afterward.

**Help desk and support teams** — Expect tickets tied to: the redesigned sharing dialog, the new Copilot entry point in Outlook, Teams' new quick-views controls in the chat list, and OneDrive's new "exclude folders from sync" option (support scripts should be updated to walk users through it). Several Teams meeting/production-control features are also mid-rollout (🚀 status = "Rolling out"), so front-line staff may see the new controls before official documentation catches up tenant-wide.

**Business owners preparing communications or training** — Focus messaging on the ⭐-marked items plus anything in your org's primary workflow apps (Teams, Outlook, SharePoint). Several Copilot features (Power BI grounding, deep citations, session/response sharing, rich answer cards) expand what end users can do with Copilot without any admin action required — worth calling out in a "what's new" note so licensed users actually discover them.
