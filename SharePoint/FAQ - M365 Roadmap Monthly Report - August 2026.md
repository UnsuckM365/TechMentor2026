# FAQ: Microsoft 365 Roadmap Monthly Report — August 2026

## 1. What are the top Microsoft 365 updates this month?
The highlighted updates are SharePoint admin move for Viva Connections home sites, Planner tabs in private and shared Teams channels, Purview DLP AI Policy Optimizer preview, Copilot Rich Answer Cards, and Teams Rooms gallery modernization.

## 2. How many roadmap items are included in the August 2026 report?
The report includes **9** Microsoft 365 roadmap items.

## 3. Which updates are in General Availability?
The General Availability items are:
- Copilot Rich Answer Cards
- Multimodal capture in Copilot Notebooks
- MCP Agents with Interactive UI Widgets in Government Clouds
- Viva Connections multi-home site management in SharePoint Admin Center
- Modernized Gallery view in Teams Rooms on Android
- Planner tab support for shared and private Teams channels

## 4. Which updates are in Preview?
The Preview items are:
- DLP AI Powered Policy Optimizer
- Archive action for OneDrive and SharePoint files

## 5. What should SharePoint admins pay attention to?
SharePoint admins should watch the move of Viva Connections multi-home site management into the SharePoint Admin Center and review related admin runbooks, governance processes, and communication plans.

## 6. What is changing for Viva Connections home sites?
Multi-home site management for Viva Connections is moving into the SharePoint Admin Center, giving SharePoint and Viva admins a more centralized management experience.

## 7. What is changing in Microsoft Planner and Teams?
Planner tabs can be added to shared and private Teams channels, expanding task management into more collaboration spaces.

## 8. What is the Purview DLP AI Powered Policy Optimizer?
It is a Microsoft Purview preview feature that suggests AI-powered improvements for Data Loss Prevention policies. It mainly affects compliance and security admins.

## 9. What is the new OneDrive and SharePoint archive action?
Purview retention policies are gaining archive actions that use Microsoft 365 Archive. This affects compliance, records, SharePoint, and OneDrive admins.

## 10. What are Copilot Rich Answer Cards?
Copilot Rich Answer Cards add richer, more useful answer formats across Copilot experiences for Copilot users and adoption teams.

## 11. What is multimodal capture in Copilot Notebooks?
It lets users capture audio, images, and notes, then generate structured notes. It affects knowledge workers and Copilot users.

## 12. What is changing for government cloud Copilot users?
MCP Agents with Interactive UI Widgets are reaching General Availability in government clouds, enabling richer Copilot Chat agent experiences.

## 13. What is changing in Teams Rooms on Android?
Teams Rooms on Android is getting a modernized gallery view with improved layouts and admin controls for meeting rooms.

## 14. Are all release dates precise?
No. Most dates are month-level only. One item, “Ask Copilot about an image,” has unclear or conflicting August–September 2026 timing.

## 15. What actions should admins take next?
Admins should validate licensing, review configuration settings, monitor Message Center and roadmap updates, prepare end-user communications, review governance policies, and update support or adoption materials.
